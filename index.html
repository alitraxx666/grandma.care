Senior Tablet Caregiver Dashboard

Get started

Set up the caregiver dashboard first, then open the senior tablet screen on the device the senior will use.

1
Open the caregiver dashboard

Enter the senior’s name, caregiver name, family note, medications, meals, appointments, and daily routines.

2
Save the schedule to the cloud

The dashboard saves the reminder schedule so the senior tablet can update from another device.

3
Open the senior tablet screen

Use Safari Add to Home Screen on iPad, or a kiosk browser on Android, to make the tablet feel full-screen.

4
Let the senior respond

The senior can press “Yes, I did it” or “I need help,” giving families a simple way to check in.

The Story Behind Grandma.care

Grandma.care was inspired by Barbara, my grandmother living independently with dementia. My family wanted a better way to help her stay safe, remember prescriptions, keep appointments, and follow daily routines without taking away her independence before it was necessary.

The goal is not more technology. The goal is simple daily support that feels familiar, dignified, and reassuring.
