Senior Tablet Caregiver Dashboard
For analog people in a digital world.

Simple support for daily independence.

Grandma.Care is a caregiver dashboard and senior tablet system designed for older adults who may struggle with memory, routines, medications, or modern technology. A caregiver sets up reminders from one device, and the senior sees a calm, easy tablet screen with family photos, a large clock, clear prompts, and simple buttons to say “I did it” or “I need help.”

Get Started

Start with the caregiver dashboard. Then connect the senior tablet so reminders, photos, and messages appear automatically.

The Story Behind Grandma.Care

Grandma.Care was inspired by Barbara, my grandmother living independently with dementia. My family wanted a better way to help her stay safe, remember prescriptions, keep appointments, and follow daily routines without taking away her independence before it was necessary.

The goal is not more technology. The goal is simple daily support that feels familiar, dignified, and reassuring.
